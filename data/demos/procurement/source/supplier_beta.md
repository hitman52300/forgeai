# Supplier Information: Beta Shipcare Components (Supplier ID SUP-B)

Purchasing department supplier file. Last reviewed April 2026. All information is fictional.

## 1. Supplier Profile

1.1 Beta Shipcare Components is a marine spare parts distributor based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Its product focus is main and auxiliary engine spares, pumps, purifiers, valves, electrical, navigation, deck machinery and safety equipment, with an emphasis on container ships.

1.2 Supplier ID in our systems: SUP-B. Quotations are requested by email to the Beta inside-sales team.

## 2. Commercial Terms

2.1 Currency: all quotations and invoices are in euro (EUR).

2.2 Payment terms: 30 days from the invoice date.

2.3 Minimum order value: EUR 1,000 per purchase order.

2.4 Quotation validity: prices quoted are valid for 60 days from the quotation date.

2.5 Incoterms: EXW Rotterdam unless the purchase order states otherwise.

## 3. Warranty and Returns

3.1 Warranty: 18 months from the date of delivery or 12 months from installation, whichever comes first.

3.2 Returns: unused parts in original packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery.

3.3 Restocking fee: 10% of the invoice value of returned parts. Safety equipment cannot be returned once the seal is broken.

## 4. Quality and Certification

4.1 Parts are supplied with a certificate of conformity. Original maker's certificates are available on request for an administration fee.

4.2 Safety equipment is supplied with valid type-approval (wheelmark) certificates.

## 5. Product Information and Specifications

5.1 SP-1001 Main Engine Fuel Injector Nozzle (FIN-58-220). Pattern part manufactured to the maker's drawing: 9 spray holes of 0.65 mm, opening pressure 420 bar. Supplied with a certificate of conformity, not a maker's certificate.

5.2 SP-2001 Auxiliary Engine Fuel Pump Element (AFP-E-12). Plunger and barrel assembly, 12 mm plunger diameter, matched pair.

5.3 SP-3001 Centrifugal Seawater Pump Impeller (CSP-IMP-150). Nickel-aluminium bronze impeller, 280 mm diameter, for pumps rated 180 m3/h at 3.5 bar.

5.4 SP-3002 Pump Mechanical Seal 45mm (MS-45-SIC). Silicon carbide faces, EPDM elastomers, shaft 45 mm, maximum pressure 10 bar.

5.5 SP-5001 Butterfly Valve DN150 PN16 (BFV-150-16). Wafer type, ductile iron body, stainless steel disc, EPDM liner, manual lever operation.

5.6 SP-5003 Safety Relief Valve (SRV-10-25). Available in two set-pressure variants, 7 bar and 10 bar, under the same part reference. The quotation confirms which variant is supplied.

5.7 SP-9001 Lifejacket SOLAS Adult (LJ-SOL-A). SOLAS and MED approved, buoyancy 150 N, with light and whistle.

5.8 SP-9101 Fuel Oil Duplex Filter Element 25 micron (FOF-25). Pleated stainless steel mesh, 25 micron nominal rating, maximum differential pressure 5 bar.
