# Supplier Information: Alpha Marine Spares (Supplier ID SUP-A)

Purchasing department supplier file. Last reviewed March 2026. All information is fictional.

## 1. Supplier Profile

1.1 Alpha Marine Spares is a marine spare parts stockist based in Singapore. Its product focus is main engine, auxiliary engine, pump, purifier, valve, electrical and navigation spares for bulk carriers and tankers.

1.2 Supplier ID in our systems: SUP-A. Contact for quotations: the Alpha sales desk via the supplier portal.

## 2. Commercial Terms

2.1 Currency: all quotations and invoices are in US dollars (USD).

2.2 Payment terms: 45 days from the invoice date.

2.3 Minimum order value: USD 500 per purchase order.

2.4 Quotation validity: prices quoted are valid for 30 days from the quotation date.

2.5 Incoterms: FCA Singapore unless the purchase order states otherwise.

## 3. Warranty and Returns

3.1 Warranty: 12 months from the date of delivery for manufacturing defects.

3.2 Returns: unused parts in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

3.3 Restocking fee: 15% of the invoice value of returned parts. Parts specially ordered for us (availability "On Request") cannot be returned.

## 4. Quality and Certification

4.1 Each part is supplied with a maker's certificate or a certificate of conformity.

4.2 Parts for class-relevant systems are supplied with class approval documentation where the maker provides it. Alpha does not issue class certificates itself.

## 5. Product Information and Specifications

5.1 SP-1001 Main Engine Fuel Injector Nozzle (maker part FIN-58-220). Nozzle with 9 spray holes of 0.65 mm, opening pressure 420 bar, material heat-treated tool steel. Supplied in sets of one; recommended overhaul interval 8,000 running hours.

5.2 SP-1004 Piston Ring Set (PRS-500). Set of 5 rings for one cylinder of 500 mm bore: 1 top ring with chrome-ceramic coating, 3 compression rings, 1 oil scraper ring.

5.3 SP-3001 Centrifugal Seawater Pump Impeller (CSP-IMP-150). Bronze impeller, 150 mm outlet, 280 mm diameter, for pumps rated 180 m3/h at 3.5 bar.

5.4 SP-3002 Pump Mechanical Seal 45mm (MS-45-SIC). Silicon carbide / silicon carbide faces, Viton elastomers, shaft 45 mm, maximum pressure 16 bar, temperature range -20 to 120 degrees C.

5.5 SP-4002 Purifier Gravity Disc Kit (PGD-K-30). Kit of 12 gravity discs with inner diameters from 60 mm to 82 mm in 2 mm steps, stainless steel.

5.6 SP-5003 Safety Relief Valve 10 bar (SRV-10-25). Set pressure 10 bar, DN25 inlet, bronze body, spring-loaded, tested and sealed.

5.7 SP-6002 Generator Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR-G-440). For 440 V, 60 Hz brushless generators, voltage regulation accuracy plus or minus 1%.

5.8 SP-9101 Fuel Oil Duplex Filter Element 25 micron (FOF-25). Pleated stainless steel mesh, 25 micron nominal rating, maximum differential pressure 6 bar.
