# Coral Harbour Marine Services - Attendance and Workplace Policy (Shore Staff)

Version 1.9, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources Department.

## Part A - Attendance

### 1. Working Hours

1.1 Office hours are 09:30 to 17:30, Monday to Friday. Core hours, during which all office staff must be present, are 10:00 to 16:00.

1.2 The lunch break is 45 minutes, to be taken between 12:30 and 14:30.

### 2. Recording Attendance

2.1 Attendance is recorded by biometric punch-in and punch-out at the office entrance, which is synchronised with HRMS. Port and terminal staff without a biometric device punch in through the HRMS mobile application.

2.2 A missed punch must be regularised in HRMS within 3 working days, with the approval of the reporting manager. A missed punch that is not regularised is treated as half a day of absence.

### 3. Late Arrival

3.1 A grace period of 10 minutes applies: arrival up to 09:40 is not marked late.

3.2 Arrival after 09:40 is recorded as a late mark. Three late marks in a calendar month are counted as half a day of annual leave, deducted automatically in HRMS.

### 4. Work From Home

4.1 Confirmed employees in office roles may work from home for up to 4 days per calendar month, with prior approval of the reporting manager in HRMS.

4.2 Employees on probation and port or terminal staff are not eligible for work from home.

### 5. Extra Hours and Compensatory Off

5.1 Work on a weekend or public holiday that is requested in writing by the reporting manager earns one day of compensatory off for each full day worked.

5.2 Compensatory off must be availed within 60 days of the day worked, otherwise it lapses.

## Part B - Workplace Rules

### 6. Dress Code

6.1 The office dress code is business casual from Monday to Thursday and smart casual on Friday.

6.2 At port and terminal sites, safety shoes, a high-visibility vest and a safety helmet are mandatory at all times.

### 7. Port Site Safety

7.1 Every employee must complete the port site safety induction before the first visit to any port or terminal site. The induction is valid for 2 years.

### 8. IT and Email Use

8.1 Company email and systems are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or security.

8.2 Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days. Passwords must never be shared.

8.3 Only company-approved devices may connect to the company network. Lost devices must be reported to IT within 4 hours.

### 9. Prevention of Harassment

9.1 The company does not tolerate harassment of any kind. An Internal Committee (IC) handles complaints of sexual harassment.

9.2 A complaint may be submitted in writing to the IC by email or through HRMS within 3 months of the incident.

9.3 The IC completes its inquiry within 90 days of receiving the complaint. Retaliation against a complainant or witness is a disciplinary offence.

### 10. Smoking and Alcohol

10.1 Smoking is allowed only in designated outdoor areas. Smoking is prohibited at all port and terminal sites.

10.2 Consumption of alcohol or being under its influence during working hours or on company premises is prohibited.

### 11. Visitors

11.1 Visitors must be registered at reception, wear a visitor badge and be accompanied by the host employee at all times.

## Part C - Disciplinary Process

### 12. Steps

12.1 Minor breaches of this policy are handled in three steps: a verbal warning, then a written warning, and then a formal inquiry by HR.

12.2 Serious misconduct, such as harassment, theft or working under the influence of alcohol, may lead directly to a formal inquiry and suspension pending inquiry.

12.3 The employee has the right to be heard and to submit a written explanation within 5 working days of receiving a written warning or inquiry notice.
