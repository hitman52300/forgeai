# Coral Harbour Marine Services - Employee Handbook (Shore Staff)

Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources Department.

## 1. About this Handbook

1.1 This handbook applies to all shore staff of Coral Harbour Marine Services, including office staff at the head office and staff working at port and terminal offices. It does not apply to seafarers serving on board managed vessels, who are covered by their contracts of employment and the applicable collective agreements.

1.2 The handbook must be read together with the Leave Policy and the Attendance and Workplace Policy. Where this handbook and a specific policy differ, the specific policy prevails.

1.3 The HR Management System (HRMS) is the single system of record for employee data, leave and attendance. Employees access HRMS with their company login.

## 2. Employment Basics

2.1 Probation. All new shore staff serve a probation period of six months from the date of joining. The reporting manager may extend probation once, by up to three months, with written reasons.

2.2 Confirmation. Confirmation of employment is communicated by a confirmation letter issued by HR. An employee is not confirmed until this letter has been issued.

2.3 Notice period. During probation, either party may end employment with 15 days' written notice. After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for all grades. The notice period may be shortened only with the written approval of the department head and HR.

2.4 Employee ID. Every employee receives an employee ID on the first day. The ID is used for HRMS, the access card and all HR correspondence.

## 3. Working Week

3.1 The standard working week for shore staff is Monday to Friday, 09:30 to 17:30, a total of 40 hours per week including breaks as defined in the Attendance and Workplace Policy.

3.2 Port and terminal staff may be placed on a published duty roster. Roster staff work the same 40 weekly hours, distributed according to the roster.

## 4. Employee Procedures

4.1 Updating personal details. Employees must keep their address, emergency contact and bank details up to date in HRMS under "My Profile". Changes to legal name or date of birth require a supporting document uploaded to HRMS and verification by HR.

4.2 Employment verification letter. Requests for an employment verification letter are raised in HRMS under "HR Requests". HR issues the letter within 3 working days of the request.

4.3 Grievances. An employee who has a work-related grievance should follow these steps:
(a) Raise the matter informally with the reporting manager.
(b) If unresolved, submit a written grievance to HR through the HRMS grievance form.
(c) HR acknowledges the grievance within 2 working days and provides a written response within 10 working days.
(d) If the employee is not satisfied, the employee may appeal to the Head of HR within 5 working days of the response. The appeal decision is final.
Grievances related to harassment follow the separate process in the Attendance and Workplace Policy.

4.4 Exit procedure. An employee leaving the company must complete a handover note approved by the reporting manager and complete the exit clearance form in HRMS. The clearance form requires sign-off from IT (return of laptop and access card), Finance and the reporting manager. The relieving letter is issued after clearance is complete.

## 5. Code of Conduct Summary

5.1 Conflicts of interest. Employees must declare any personal, family or financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor to HR in writing as soon as it arises.

5.2 Gifts. Employees may accept gifts from business contacts only if the value does not exceed INR 2,000 per gift. Any gift above this value must be declined or handed over to HR. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts must never be accepted.

5.3 Confidentiality. Company, vessel and customer information must not be shared outside the company without authorisation. This obligation continues after employment ends.

## 6. Learning and Development

6.1 Each shore employee is expected to complete at least 24 hours of training per calendar year. Training completed is recorded in HRMS.

6.2 Mandatory courses (data protection, anti-bribery and workplace conduct) must be completed within 30 days of joining.

## 7. Compensation and Benefits

7.1 Salary, payroll processing, allowances, bonuses, insurance and other benefits are covered by the separate Compensation and Benefits Policy. That policy is not part of this handbook. Employees should contact HR for any question about compensation or benefits.

## 8. Changes to this Handbook

8.1 HR reviews this handbook annually. Changes are announced by email and published in HRMS.
