# Coral Harbour Marine Services - Business Travel Policy (Shore Staff)

Version 1.0, effective 1 July 2026. Owner: Human Resources Department. (Fictional.)

## 1. Approval

1.1 All business travel must be approved in HRMS by the reporting manager before any booking is made.

1.2 International travel additionally requires the approval of the department head.

## 2. Booking

2.1 Flights and hotels are booked only through the company travel desk, at least 10 working days before departure where possible.

2.2 Economy class is used for flights of less than 6 hours.

## 3. Travel to Port Sites

3.1 Employees travelling to a port or terminal site must have a valid port site safety induction, as required by the Attendance and Workplace Policy.

## 4. After the Trip

4.1 A trip report must be submitted in HRMS within 5 working days of return.
