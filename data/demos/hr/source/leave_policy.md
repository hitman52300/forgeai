# Coral Harbour Marine Services - Leave Policy (Shore Staff)

Version 2.4, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources Department.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all shore staff. It is read together with the Employee Handbook and the Attendance and Workplace Policy.

1.2 The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. All leave is applied for and approved in HRMS.

## 2. Accrual for New Joiners

2.1 Employees who join during the year receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis of 2 days for every completed month of service in that leave year.

2.2 Sick leave and casual leave for new joiners are also granted pro-rata, rounded down to the nearest whole day.

## 3. Annual Leave

3.1 Confirmed and probationary employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per leave year.

3.2 Annual leave must be applied for in HRMS at least 7 calendar days before the first day of leave. The reporting manager approves or rejects the request in HRMS.

3.3 Annual leave of more than 10 consecutive working days requires the additional approval of the department head.

## 4. Carry Forward and Lapse

4.1 A maximum of 15 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next leave year.

4.2 Unused annual leave above 15 days lapses on 31 December and is not compensated.

## 5. Leave Encashment

5.1 Annual leave cannot be encashed during employment.

5.2 At separation, accumulated annual leave of up to 30 days may be encashed. The amount is calculated under the Compensation and Benefits Policy.

## 6. Sick Leave

6.1 Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per leave year.

6.2 A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. The certificate must be uploaded to HRMS within 3 working days of returning to work.

6.3 Sick leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed.

## 7. Casual Leave

7.1 Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per leave year.

7.2 A maximum of 2 days of casual leave may be taken at one time.

7.3 Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave in the same continuous period of absence.

## 8. Parental Leave

8.1 Maternity leave. Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave for each of the first two children, and 12 weeks for a third or later child.

8.2 Paternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

## 9. Bereavement Leave

9.1 Employees are entitled to 5 working days of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent or sibling).

## 10. Public Holidays

10.1 Shore staff receive 12 public holidays per calendar year. HR publishes the holiday list for the following year in HRMS each December.

## 11. Leave Without Pay

11.1 Leave without pay applies only when all applicable paid leave has been exhausted, and requires the approval of the department head and HR. Leave without pay may not exceed 30 days in a leave year.

## 12. Sandwich Rule

12.1 When annual leave is taken on both the Friday before and the Monday after a weekend, the intervening Saturday and Sunday are counted as annual leave.

## 13. Emergency Leave Procedure

13.1 In an emergency, the employee must inform the reporting manager by phone or email within 2 hours of the normal start time.

13.2 The leave must be regularised by applying in HRMS within 2 working days of returning to work. Unregularised absence is treated as leave without pay.
