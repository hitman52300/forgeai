Write a leave policy (about 1,300 words) for the shore staff of the fictional company
"Coral Harbour Marine Services". Use numbered section headings.

Include specific, internally consistent values for:
- Leave year (January to December) and pro-rata accrual for joiners (state the rule,
  e.g. per completed month).
- Annual (earned) leave: days per year, minimum notice, approval by reporting manager
  in HRMS, maximum consecutive days without department-head approval.
- Carry forward: maximum days that can be carried into the next year; lapse rule.
- Leave encashment: only at separation, maximum days, (do not state amounts of money).
- Sick leave: days per year, medical certificate requirement after N consecutive days,
  no carry forward.
- Casual leave: days per year, maximum at one time, cannot be combined with annual leave.
- Maternity leave (weeks) and paternity leave (days, within how many months of birth).
- Bereavement leave (days).
- Public holidays: number per year, list published by HR each December.
- Leave without pay: when it applies.
- Sandwich rule: weekends between leave days.
- Procedure: apply in HRMS, emergency leave informed by phone/email within a stated
  number of hours and regularised in HRMS within a stated number of days.
Do not mention salary amounts or benefits other than leave.
