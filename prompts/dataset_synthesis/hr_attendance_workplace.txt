Write an "Attendance and Workplace Policy" (about 1,300 words) for the shore staff of the
fictional company "Coral Harbour Marine Services". Use numbered section headings.

Part A - Attendance (consistent with the handbook: 09:30-17:30, Monday-Friday):
- core hours, lunch break length, grace period for late arrival (minutes),
- biometric/HRMS punch-in rule, missed-punch regularisation deadline,
- late-mark rule (how many late marks in a month count as half a day of annual leave),
- work-from-home: eligibility after probation, days per month, approval,
- overtime/extra hours: compensatory off rule (no payment amounts).
Part B - Workplace rules:
- dress code (business casual; safety shoes and helmet at port/terminal sites),
- port site safety induction requirement before first visit,
- IT and email acceptable use, password and device rules,
- prevention of harassment: Internal Committee, how to report, timeline for inquiry,
- smoking and alcohol rules,
- visitor policy.
Part C - Disciplinary process: steps (verbal warning, written warning, inquiry).
Do not mention salary amounts. Keep values consistent with the leave policy where they
interact (late marks are deducted from annual leave).
