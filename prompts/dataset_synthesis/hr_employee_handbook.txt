Write an employee handbook (about 1,300 words) for the shore (office and port) staff of a
fictional ship-management company called "Coral Harbour Marine Services".

Use numbered section headings. Cover:
1. About the handbook: who it applies to (shore staff, not seafarers on board), that it
   is read together with the Leave Policy and the Attendance and Workplace Policy.
2. Employment basics: probation period (state a length), confirmation by letter, notice
   periods during and after probation (state values).
3. Working week: office days and the standard weekly hours (consistent with the
   attendance policy: 09:30-17:30 Monday to Friday, 40 hours).
4. Employee procedures: how to update personal details in HRMS, how to request an
   employment verification letter (state a turnaround time), how to raise a
   grievance (steps and response time), exit procedure (handover, clearance form).
5. Code of conduct summary: conflicts of interest, gifts (state a value limit in INR),
   confidentiality.
6. Learning and development: a yearly training-hours expectation.
7. A short statement that salary, payroll, allowances and benefits are covered by a
   separate Compensation and Benefits Policy that is NOT part of this handbook, and
   that employees should contact HR for it. Do not state any salary or benefit details.

Write in a formal, internally consistent style. Do not include real company names.
